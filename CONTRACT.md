# C5 Coach's Co-pilot: API contract

Base URL (deployed): `https://coach.raava.dev` (also `https://coach-copilot-clef.zay-259.workers.dev`).
Requests and responses are JSON. The examples below are real responses from that deployment, generated by `scripts/contract.py`; long arrays are trimmed with `"…"` and timings with `"ms": "…"`.
The calls and people are synthetic (Crane Lab hackathon set). Nobody in them is real.

The console (Patient 360) is the user-facing app. This Worker is the engine: Clef tags each line, GLM-5.3-flash reviews unsure tags and writes cues, and the suite endpoints serve the labelled call set.

## Errors and limits

| Status | Body | When |
|---|---|---|
| 400 | `{"error": "line must be {speaker: coach|participant, text}"}` | bad `line` (speaker must be `coach` or `participant`; text 1–1,000 chars) |
| 404 | `{"error": "call not found", "callId": "CALL99999"}` | unknown `callId` / `survivorId` |
| 429 | `{"error":"rate_limited"}`, header `retry-after: 20` | Workers AI per-minute quota spent (error 3021). `/api/turn` waits ~0.5 s, then ~1.5 s, before answering 429. `/api/review` and `/api/cue` back off and retry twice (~2 s, then ~5 s) first. Keep the Clef tag and skip the review or cue. |
| 502 | `{"error":"…"}` | upstream model error after one retry |

Workers AI quotas are per account and model, shared by every caller of this Worker (coach.raava.dev, Patient 360, batch scripts).

## `POST /api/turn`: tag one line (every line; Clef call ~0.3–0.5 s p50, round trip longer: see `scripts/e2e_live.py`)

Body: `line` = `{speaker, text}`, `previous` = up to the 2 lines before it. Pass each previous line's `flag` as returned; a coach line right after a red flag (`medical`, `distress`, `social`) is then also judged for routing.

- `tag`: coach lines, one of `open question`, `closed question`, `simple reflection`, `complex reflection`, `affirm`, `seek collaboration`, `emphasize autonomy`, `ask permission`, `give information`, `persuade`, `confront`, `other`. Participant lines: `change`, `sustain`, `neutral`.
- `confidence`: Clef's confidence. Below 0.3 (about 25% of lines, 76% accurate), send the line to `/api/review`. At 0.3 or above, it is 98% accurate on dev.
- `flag`: participant lines get `none | medical | distress | social | dropout`. This is judged on the line alone, so earlier lines cannot leak in. A model-free red-flag screen (`src/redflags.json`: explicit suicidal ideation, suicide warning signs, breathing or chest pain, bleeding; English, Spanish, Haitian Creole) runs first. On a hit, the response also carries `rule` (the rule id) and `modelFlag` (what the model said), and `flag` is that rule's red kind. Every `distress` flag also carries `action: "Use your suicide-risk screening protocol now."`, because the flag misses some indirect warning signs. Coach lines get `none | missed_concern | medical_advice`.
  - Red, act today: `medical`, `distress`, `social`.
  - Amber: `dropout`, `missed_concern` (the coach did not acknowledge or route a red flag), `medical_advice`.

## `POST /api/review`: second opinion on an unsure tag (async, ~2–4 s)

Answers are cached for 7 days by model and prompt. A repeat returns at once with `"cached": true` and `"ms": 0`.

Body: `line`, `previous` and `probabilities` from `/api/turn`. GLM picks among Clef's top 4 coach labels (all 3 participant labels). `label` is `null` when it gives no valid answer; keep Clef's tag then. On 600 validation lines below 0.3 confidence the review moves accuracy by under one point (see README), so it is optional.

```json
{
  "ms": "…",
  "label": "affirm",
  "reason": "The coach praises the participant's effort and persistence ('kept going even with sore knees'), which is direct praise of an achievement, not merely a paraphrase of what the participant said.",
  "cached": true
}
```

## `POST /api/cue`: one coaching cue (async, ~2–5 s)

Cached like `/api/review`: the same trigger and lines return the stored cue with `"cached": true`.

Body: `trigger` (why, max 300 chars) and `recent` (up to 8 lines, with `tag`/`flag` when known). `cue` is English; `say` is in the call's language, detected from the participant's words. Either may be `null` on a model miss.

```json
{
  "trigger": "Participant raised a medical concern.",
  "recent": [
    {
      "speaker": "coach",
      "text": "How did the walking go this week?"
    },
    {
      "speaker": "participant",
      "text": "I walked three days, but my knees hurt after."
    },
    {
      "speaker": "participant",
      "text": "Oh, before you go, I've had a fever since last night. I'm still on chemo.",
      "flag": "medical"
    }
  ]
}
```

```json
{
  "ms": "…",
  "cue": "Shift out of the coaching flow: acknowledge the fever, and without diagnosing, encourage them to contact their oncology care team now — this is a safety priority over the walking topic. Then invite them to decide next step.",
  "say": "Thanks for telling me — a fever during chemo is something your care team will want to hear about right away. What's the quickest way you can reach them today?",
  "cached": true
}
```

Trigger rules used by the demo page (the console reuses them): confront, persuasion nobody asked for, missed change talk, four questions without a reflection, three closed questions in a row, any flag. After a cue, wait three coach lines before the next.

- `banners[]`: each line whose flag raises a banner, with `level` (`red` or `amber`) and display text.
- `cues[]`: each GLM cue, with the line it follows and the trigger.

- `tagAgreement`: share of lines where Clef's tag matches the human label.

## `GET /api/c5/agreement`: coach score vs supervisors

From `scripts/agreement.py`, on the supervisor-rated validation calls.
- `correlations[]`: Pearson and Spearman with bootstrap 95% CIs, for each pair: app vs supervisor A, app vs B, app vs the mean, A vs B, human-label score vs the mean.
- `supervisorIcc`: ICC(2,1) for A vs B.
- `likeForLike`: r(app, A) − r(B, A) and r(app, B) − r(A, B), with paired-bootstrap CIs. Each supervisor is compared with the other supervisor, not with the smoother mean.
- `crossFittedIcc`: ICC(2,1) of the app score, mapped to 1–5 by a line fitted on the other folds, against A and against B, next to A vs B.
- `byLanguage`: the same correlations for English and Spanish calls.
- `tagAgreement`: accuracy and Cohen's kappa for coach move and participant talk, overall and by language; `annomiKappa` on the expert-labelled AnnoMI lines.
- `safetyStress`: the independent stress sets v3 and v2 (EN/ES/Haitian Creole): urgent lines caught, by language and by kind, and hard negatives flagged, each `{k, n, rate, ci95}`.
- `cues`: cue rate per call, trigger precision and recall against the human labels, and the cue count's Spearman correlation with the supervisors' mean score.
- `mae`: the app score mapped to 1–5, then the mean absolute difference from each supervisor.
- `flags`: recall, false alarms per line and per call, and coach-conduct agreement, each `{k, n, rate, ci95}` (Wilson), plus `byLanguage`.
- `perCall[]`: one row per call, `{callId, appScore, labelScore, supervisorA, supervisorB}`.
